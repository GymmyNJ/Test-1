Common questions

Can I sign up on the website? +

No. Membership is limited. Request a spot on the Contact page. Admin creates your login when you’re accepted.

What if peak hours run out? +

You can still book off-peak (before 7 AM and after 7 PM) with no limit. Peak hours reset each calendar month.

Can two people share one membership? +

Each login is for one member. Ask us if you need a household option.

How far ahead can I book? +

Any future date on the calendar. Taken bay and time combinations are hidden so you only see what’s open.
