FAQ

What does membership include? +

$400/month includes 24/7 gym access, 21 peak simulator hours (7 AM–7 PM), unlimited off-peak simulator hours, and member-only booking of five bays. No public walk-ins.

Can I sign up on the website? +

No. The roster is capped. Request a spot on the Contact page. Only Admin can create logins after you’re accepted.

What are peak vs off-peak hours? +

Peak is 7 AM–7 PM and counts toward your 21 hours. Off-peak is before 7 AM and after 7 PM and is unlimited. On the calendar, peak times are red and off-peak times are green.

What if I use all 21 peak hours? +

You can still book off-peak with no cap. Peak hours reset on the first of each calendar month.

How does booking work? +

Members log in, pick a date, duration (1–3 hours), start time, and an open bay. Taken slots disappear so you only see what’s available. The facility is bookable 24 hours a day.

Can I bring guests? +

Guests are not allowed during peak hours (7 AM–7 PM). Off-peak only, members may bring up to 3 guests for $50 per bay. Only the member can book the bay, and the member must be present with the guests. You’ll enter each guest’s first name, last name, email, and date of birth when you book.

Who can book a bay? +

Members only. Guests cannot reserve a simulator. If you bring guests off-peak, you book and you stay for the session.

Can two people share one membership? +

Each login is one member. Ask us if you need a household option.

How far ahead can I book? +

Any future date. Past dates are locked. Cancel from your upcoming list if plans change.

How do I update my contact info? +

Sign in → My Profile. Add email, phone, and address. Admin can also see this on your account.
